Bug Out In Bag

An ever-evolving bug-in bag checklist built for natural disasters, infrastructure failure, and even the occasional alien invasion.

Warning

The purpose of this bag is to get me safely from wherever I am to my designated safe location. Outside of emergency situations, it doubles as my camping and exploration bag after removing some items to avoid having awkward conversations. This list serves as both a reference for tinkering and a checklist to ensure everything is accounted for when unpacking and repacking the bag.

I don't recommend replicating this setup exactly. This kit is tailored specifically to my needs, including my familiarity with the local terrain, local climate and the distance I need to travel on foot.

There are also a few items I keep on me, even on regular days, so they aren't included in the bags. I still need a full-face gas mask, filters, and a decent monocular, but I need to look into them first.
