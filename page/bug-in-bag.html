Bug Out In Bag

An ever-evolving bug-in bag checklist built for natural disasters, infrastructure failure, and even the occasional alien invasion.

Warning

The purpose of this bag is to get me safely from wherever I am to my designated safe location. This list serves as both a reference for tinkering and a checklist to ensure everything is accounted for when unpacking and repacking the bag.

If you're looking to replicate this setup exactly, I don't recommend it. This kit is tailored specifically to my needs, including my familiarity with the local terrain, local climate and the distance I need to travel on foot.
