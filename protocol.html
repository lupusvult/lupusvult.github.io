Protocol

A practical guide to getting along with me. If you don't fit, stay away.

Introduction

The existence of this list might sound a little harsh, or even extreme, but it's not as bad as it may seem in reality. Anyone who reads it will understand that these are actually quite reasonable and acceptable.

Nobody is forced to fit within them, we simply don't have to interact if our boundaries or expectations are incompatible. I don't expect more from others than what I'm willing to give myself, so I think it's pretty fair.

This list was forged through experiences, and I have a low tolerance level because of it. I'm carrying the title "the Bridge Burner" under my name, so doing any of the following will result in an instagib. Nothing fancy; I'll simply cut off contact instantly and permanently, even if the person did it as a joke.

Terms of Interaction

  1. I don't lend money, because people's behavior changes when you ask for it back. I also don't tolerate people trying to get money out of me or profit from my work without making a fully transparent and fair deal first.
  2. If something needs to be done, it should be done immediately. When someone deliberately drags their feet on something that involves or affects me, it bothers me. It feels like they're dumping their responsibilities on me and using me to pick up the slack.
  3. It doesn't matter how public or unimportant the information is; I don't like people giving information about me to someone else. The correct response is, "None of my business. Go ask him yourself" and leave it at that.
  4. Everyone has problems. When someone starts whining about theirs, it makes me think about the problems I'm dealing with at that moment, and time just passes while nothing gets solved. If it's not something I can actually do anything about, I'd rather not hear it. It's mentally draining.
  5. I don't care what kind of lie it is; they're not acceptable. Secrets are in the same basket. If someone knows something that will affect me in one way or another, I expect to know about it.
  6. Promising or speaking on my behalf is not acceptable. If something involves my time, work, or participation, I expect to be asked directly, without a third party getting involved.
  7. When I say I don't want to do something, I mean it. My mind isn't going to change. Asking me the same thing multiple times might seem polite to some people, but to me, it's just annoying.
  8. I don't like unpredictable situations, and I don't do things without properly planning. If someone knocks on my door without letting me know beforehand, they won't be coming in.
  9. It's better not to promise anything than to make a promise they can't keep. Plans can be made based on those promises, so breaking them can cause a domino effect.
  10. Our opinions, small-scale personal observations, and emotions mean nothing when they're presented as facts. Research and data on the subject come first. I lose my shit when people talk about something as if it's a fact without having any proof to back it up.
  11. I don't care how people devour their food, but behaviors like smacking their lips, talking with food in their mouth, and licking their fingers are disgusting and disrespectful.
