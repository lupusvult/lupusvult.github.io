Protocol

A practical guide to getting along with me. If you don't fit, stay away.

Introduction

The existence of this list might sound a little extreme, but it's not as bad as it may seem in reality. Anyone who reads it will understand that these are actually quite reasonable. Keeping it written down will also help ensure that, one day, I don't turn into someone I would hate today.

Nobody is forced to fit within them, we simply don't have to interact if our boundaries or expectations are incompatible, and I don't expect more from others than what I'm willing to give myself.

This list was forged through experiences, and I have a low tolerance level because of it. I'm carrying the title "the Bridge Burner" under my name, so doing any of the following will result in contact being cut off instantly and permanently, even if the person did it as a joke.

Terms of Interaction

  1. I don't tolerate people trying to gain financially from me or profit from my work without making a fully transparent and fair deal first. Nobody should get more than what they deserve.
  2. If something needs to be done, it should be done immediately. When someone deliberately drags their feet on something that involves or affects me, it feels like they're trying to dump their responsibilities on me.
  3. It doesn't matter how public or unimportant the information is; I don't like people giving information about me to someone else. The correct response is, "None of my business. Go ask him yourself" and leave it at that.
  4. Everyone has problems. When someone starts whining about theirs, it makes me think about the problems I'm dealing with at that moment, and time just passes while nothing gets solved. If it's not something I can actually do anything about, I'd rather not hear it.
  5. I won't respond when someone speaks to me in an ordering manner. I expect people to speak in a polite way, or if that's too difficult, to phrase the sentence as a question.
  6. I don't care what kind of lie it is; they're not acceptable. Secrets are in the same basket. If someone knows something that will affect me in one way or another, I expect to know about it.
  7. Promising or speaking on my behalf is not acceptable. If something requires my participation, I expect to be asked directly, without a third party getting involved.
  8. When I say I don't want to do something, I mean it. My mind isn't going to change. Asking me the same thing multiple times might seem polite to some people, but to me, it's just annoying.
  9. I don't like unpredictable situations, and I don't do things without properly planning. If someone knocks on my door without letting me know beforehand, they won't be coming in.
  10. It's better not to promise anything than to make a promise they can't keep. Plans can be made based on those promises, so breaking them can cause a domino effect.
  11. When money or time is going to be invested, our opinions, small-scale personal observations, and emotions mean nothing. Research and data on the subject come first.
  12. I don't care how people devour their food, but behaviors like smacking their lips, talking with food in their mouth, licking their fingers, and touching other people's food or plate are disrespectful.
