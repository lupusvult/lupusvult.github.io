Protocol

A practical guide to who I prefer not to deal with. If you don't fit the criteria, stay away.

Introduction

The existence of this list might sound a little extreme, but it's not as bad as it may seem in reality. Anyone who reads it will understand that these are actually quite reasonable, and anyone who simply minds their own business and respects the boundaries of others is already following most of them.

I have a low tolerance for bullshit, so doing any of the following will result in contact being cut off instantly and permanently, even if the person did it in a joking manner or without knowing these terms.

Terms of Interaction

  1. I don't tolerate people trying to gain anything from me or my work without being fully transparent about their intentions and agreeing to a clearly defined deal upfront.
  2. I don't like people giving any kind of information about me to someone else. It doesn't matter how insignificant or public that information is; nobody should redistribute it without my permission.
  3. Promising or speaking on my behalf is not acceptable. If something requires my participation, I expect to be asked directly, without a third party getting involved.
  4. Nosy people are a nuisance. People shouldn't ask personal questions. If someone were meant to know something, I'd have told them already. Otherwise, they're not supposed to know it yet.
  5. If something needs to be done, it should be done immediately. When someone deliberately drags their feet on their responsibility that affects me, it feels like they're trying to dump it on me.
  6. If someone knows something that could have an impact on me, I expect to be told about it.
  7. It's better not to promise anything than to make a promise that cannot be kept. Plans can be made based on those promises, so breaking them can cause a catastrophic domino effect.
  8. People who panic and overreact during bad situations only create distractions and waste time. I expect everyone to remain calm and deal with things rationally instead of worsening the situation.
  9. I plan everything and expect people to let me know things in advance. If someone knocks on my door without letting me know at least 24 hours beforehand, they won't be coming in.
  10. Everyone has issues, but constantly complaining and whining really gets on my nerves. If it's not something I can actually do anything about, I'd rather not hear about it.
  11. When I say I don't want to do something, I mean it. My mind isn't going to change. Asking me the same thing repeatedly or expecting me to give a reason is just annoying.
