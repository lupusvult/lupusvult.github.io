About this Website

It's been a long time since I last ran a website. Back then, discovering a new website was fun. They were raw and personal, designed and built from scratch, and free of bloat, aside from the occasional random GIF floating around, of course.

After the rapid rise of content management systems, people started deploying them, slapping on a theme, and calling it a day. At the same time, social media platforms took over and killed bulletin board community culture. But it didn't stop there. JavaScript was on the rise, it enabled tracking and data collection by advertising companies and three-letter agencies. The internet wasn't the good old Wild West anymore. It felt like it lost its character, and I lost my interest.

I came up with the idea for this website on April 16, 2026, while browsing Web Design Museum. They reminded me how happy I used to be designing and maintaining websites when I was younger.

Purpose

This website is a personal archive of ideas and data, my own little cozy space. My non-sensitive notes and spreadsheets are scattered everywhere, so this gives me a way to bring them all under one roof.

Social media platforms censor speech, whether due to government pressure or advertiser interests. Personal websites, on the other hand, are ungovernable. That aside, seeing people bicker about some random shit every single day is exhausting. So, I maintain a page where I share what I'm currently up to, updating it whenever life gets interesting and I remember to do so.

Design Choices

I've tried to keep everything ultra-minimalist while capturing the soul of pre-Y2K-era personal websites. It's a static website, and the entire structure branches out from the main page.

Before building it, I set a few rules and stuck to them:

  1. It must feel primitive, yet visually appealing.
  2. It must have a theme.
  3. Keep it text-oriented.
  4. Don't use web fonts.
  5. Keep CSS free of clutter.
  6. Keep pages under 100 KB.
  7. Stay stateless, no cookies.
  8. Avoid JavaScript entirely.
  9. Use no analytics, even privacy-friendly ones.

End Goal

There's no goal. I just want to optimize the shit out of it, make it as clean as possible, and keep it running. It can evolve over time, new pages might appear out of nowhere if I pick up a new hobby.

The Dream Life

At some point, I'd like to own around 10 hectares of flat land in a secluded area near the mountains. My goal would be to build a large but minimalist, low-tech concrete house with a bunker underneath and gradually develop the property into a self-sufficient, off-grid homestead with solar power, satellite internet and groundwater extraction, and enough redundancy to stay functional in SHTF situations.

I'd also like to build a sizable chicken coop and a greenhouse for food production. I would plant just enough fruit trees for myself, fill the rest of the land with olive trees, and mind my own business without being interrupted by unexpected guests or grumpy neighbors who get disturbed by muzzle report.

I would spend my days doing exactly what I'm doing right now. Working on my projects and the land, riding aimlessly, sitting around the fire and watching the night sky.

And then, out of nowhere, I would be abducted by aliens. Probably Greys, because I'm an unlucky dude, so a thin-waisted, thick-ass Nordic alien was never a possibility in the first place. When they're done with me, I would return home, adjust to life with my newly implanted anal probe(s), and carry on as if it's just another minor inconvenience. Just like I do with every other inconvenience in my life.
